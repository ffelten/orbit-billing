# Gift cards

Customers can buy a gift card for any amount and give the code to someone else.

- Customers buy a gift card for any amount and receive a code.
- Gift cards never expire.
- The buyer receives the card code by email after purchase.
- Balances are integer cents (ADR-0002).
- Partial redemption is allowed; the remainder stays on the card.
- Remove the legacy coupon module.
- Out of scope: bulk issuance for corporate customers.
